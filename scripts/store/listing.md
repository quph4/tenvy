## Product name
Tenvy IPTV Player

## Description
```
Tenvy is a free IPTV player for Windows, for the service you already have. Sign in with your Xtream Codes login or paste an M3U playlist link, and you're watching: live TV with a full programme guide, catch-up on programmes you missed, and films and series with posters, resume and auto-play of the next episode.

Built for watching on a PC: channel numbers and fast zapping with the keyboard, a mini player that stays on top, and streams that reconnect by themselves when they drop.

Tenvy is a player only. It does not include, sell or link to any channels, playlists or subscriptions. You need your own IPTV provider, and should use Tenvy only with services you have the right to watch.

No ads. No account. No tracking.
```
